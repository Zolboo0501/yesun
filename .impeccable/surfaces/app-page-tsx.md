# Home: company section (below products)

Scope: the company introduction on `/`, between the product section and Projects. It replaces the old About block on the home page; the /about page keeps its own.
Mode: Persuade.
Audience: project commissioners judging whether the company can carry their building; equipment buyers and service clients after them.
Job: understand who Есөн Бэлчир is and what it does to the air in a building, then contact an engineer.
Proof: founded 2011 under the БХБЯ special licence, 150+ installed sites, 1 800 m² factory with 12 machines, 1–3 year warranty, 24/7 service (confirmed by the client 2026-11-03); brands KANION, AUX for cooling and BALLU, SHUFT for ventilation (structured data).
Constraints: Mongolian copy; a light section after the dark product run; motion respects reduced motion; without WebGL the plain wall and all content remain.
Unresolved: none.

## Direction contract
THESIS: the room's wall is the image. Daylight from a window lands on it and the air coming in from outside is made visible in that light, so the subject (air) is shown, not illustrated. It refuses the default about block of a photo grid and stat tiles.
OWN-WORLD: the site's cool panel grey wall, ink type, one brand-blue accent, mono uppercase labels, square buttons; a sunlit window patch and moving white air streams are the only imagery.
STORY: the visitor reads who we are and since when, picks what the air should do (fresh, cool, clean) and watches the stream change on the wall, checks six proof figures, then contacts an engineer.
FIRST VIEWPORT: one section that fits a 1440x900 screen under the header. Headline and two sentences top left (no kicker); six proof figures top right; three huge selectable words (Сэлгэлт, Хөргөлт, Шүүлт) bottom left; the selected system's panel with brands and the call to action bottom right; the light patch with its air streams behind, upper middle; on phones the light lands right above the choice. The pointer stirs the air and a click sends a gust.
FORM: extension of the established home page, hero-28's window-light form recast for air; position 1 of 1; seed: none (local extension, concept-seed not run per new-work §3).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

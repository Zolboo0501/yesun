## Direction contract
THESIS: the air made into an object: translucent streams of air, each flowing its own way, that draw a figure beside each part that fits it (a skyline of the buildings fitted, a duct elbow, a fan, a warranty shield with a check). As the company story scrolls past, the streams run as one current to the next part and redraw themselves as its figure. It refuses the default about block of a photo grid and stat tiles.
OWN-WORLD: a cool mist-grey ground (#aeb2b8) with a soft light from the lower right, ink type, one brand-blue accent (the text selection only; the air is white), mono uppercase labels, square buttons; the air streams are the only imagery.
STORY: four scroll steps. Who we are (2011, the БХБЯ licence, 150+ sites); design and our own factory (1 800 m², 12 machines); six official brands supplied and installed in the capital and the provinces; the 1–3 year warranty and 24/7 service, then the call to an engineer.
FIRST VIEWPORT: part 1 fills the stage under the header. The streams are gathered in the left half; the right half has the h2 with one sentence and two figures (no kicker). The four parts scroll down as blocks over a sticky scene and alternate sides (copy right, left, right, left). Each part's copy fades in as it reaches the stage and out as it leaves, while the streams run as one current downhill from the part being left to the next part's free half (upper left to lower right, then back). On phones the streams gather above the copy.
FORM: user-specified on 2026-11-04 (four parts stacked down the page; air streams moving like components/react-bits/chrome-orbit.tsx, each stream on its own path to one place); replaces the earlier window-light wall and the chrome orbit.

# Есөн Бэлчир website

## What the company does (the client's correction, 2026-11-15)

- The company **does not design** ventilation or cooling systems. It does not draw up the design (зураг төсөл) and does not do the design calculations (тооцоолол).
- It **installs systems to the project's approved design drawings** (зураг төслийн дагуу угсралт). It also:
  - makes ducts and fittings in its own factory;
  - supplies the equipment;
  - commissions the systems (тохируулга);
  - services them afterwards.
- Never write copy that says the company designs, calculates, or does everything "from design to service" (зураг төслөөс … хүртэл). Write "installs to the design" (зураг төслийн дагуу угсарна) instead.

## How the copy is written (the user's rule, 2026-11-15)

Every text on the site says what the client's own texts on the `main` branch say (the user's rule, 2026-11-15: express them briefly and clearly), written for a visitor who is not an engineer:
- plain, correct Mongolian, short and clear: one idea per sentence, one or two sentences per block;
- sounds like a person from the company speaking ("Бид …"), not like generated text;
- keep the client's own phrases where they carry its message, but add no empty praise of our own ("бүхий л", "цогц шийдэл", "дэвшилтэт", "тэргүүлэгч");
- only the client's facts (PRODUCT.md and `main`); never bring back what `main` claims and the client has since corrected (design and calculation), or `main`'s unconfirmed figures and testimonials ("50+ баг", "98% ханамж", the reviews).

- never show these figures anywhere on the site, in a figure block or in a sentence (the user's call, 2026-11-15), even though `main` and PRODUCT.md have them: "150+ / 150 гаруй объект", "1 800 м²" (the factory's area), "216 м²" (the warehouse), "12" (the machines), the factory's "72 × 24 м" and "7,35 м".

Use the plain word, not the trade term:

| Trade term | Write |
|---|---|
| диффузор, сараалж | агаар түгээгч |
| рекуператор | дулаан хэмнэх төхөөрөмж |
| агаар боловсруулах төхөөрөмж | агаар бэлтгэх төхөөрөмж |
| фасон эдлэл | холбох хэсэг (тохой, салаа) |
| шугам хоолой (for air) | агаарын суваг, суваг |
| конденсац | хөлрөлт |
| даралтын алдагдал | leave it out, or say "агаар жигд урсана" |

Product names from the client's catalogue (Спираль хоолой, Нарийсгагч, …) and model names stay as they are.

The client's confirmed facts and figures are in PRODUCT.md, and the design system is in DESIGN.md.
